What bridal services can I ask about?

Public service listings mention bridal and party makeup, hair styling, draping and lashes. Share your event details to confirm the exact services available for your booking.

Can I ask about makeup at my venue?

A public bridal listing mentions salon and venue services. Please ask directly about travel availability, location and any related arrangements for your date.

What should I share for a bridal enquiry?

Your event date, venue or salon preference, the type of event, number of people and a few inspiration images are useful starting points.
